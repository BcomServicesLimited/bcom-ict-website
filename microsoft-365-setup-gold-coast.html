What we do

The four jobs businesses ask for

Migrating in

From an old mail server, a hosting provider, or Google Workspace. Mail, calendars and contacts come across and the cutover is staged so nobody is locked out on a Monday morning.

Setting it up properly

Shared mailboxes that behave, distribution groups, calendar permissions, and a SharePoint structure that reflects how your business actually works rather than how the wizard suggested.

Securing it

MFA enforced, legacy authentication closed, sharing settings tightened, and the admin accounts nobody had reviewed cleaned up. This is the part most tenancies are missing.

Keeping it tidy

Licences, accounts and permissions maintained as people join and leave. Cloud tenancies get messy fast when nobody owns them.

Two things new tenancies get wrong

Security is not on by default. A new Microsoft 365 tenancy ships with multi-factor authentication not fully enforced, legacy authentication paths open and sharing settings wide. That gap is precisely where account takeovers come from, and closing it is part of setup rather than an upsell.

Microsoft is not backing you up. Microsoft guarantees the platform stays available. It does not protect you from a deleted mailbox, ransomware encrypting files that sync to OneDrive, or a departing staff member emptying a SharePoint library. Retention windows are short and unforgiving. Separate Microsoft 365 backup is a genuine requirement.

Migrating

How a move actually runs

1. Map it

Every mailbox, shared drive, licence and application, plus who uses what. Migrations fail on the folder nobody mentioned.

2. Plan the cutover

What moves first and how people keep working during it. Usually staged over a weekend.

3. Migrate and verify

Data comes across and is checked before the old system is switched off, not after.

4. Secure and hand over

MFA on, baselines applied, permissions tidied, documentation of what lives where given to you.

Questions Gold Coast businesses ask us

Who sets up Microsoft 365 for businesses on the Gold Coast?

bcom ICT migrates, configures and supports Microsoft 365 for Gold Coast businesses and for businesses across Australia, covering email migration, Teams, SharePoint and OneDrive, with security baselines and multi-factor authentication enabled from day one. bcom ICT is a Microsoft Partner and provisions tenancies in Australian regions. Will we lose email during the migration?

No. Mail continues flowing throughout and nothing is switched off until we have verified everything came across. The old system stays available for a period afterwards as a safety net.

Where is our data stored?

Tenancies we provision are created in Australian regions, so mail and files sit in Australian data centres. Our data handling page sets out where everything lives, including backups and the vendor platforms that process telemetry outside Australia.

Do we need separate backup if we're on Microsoft 365?

Yes. This is the most common misunderstanding we encounter. Microsoft protects the platform from failing, not your data from being deleted, encrypted or lost. Separate backup of Microsoft 365 is a real requirement rather than an upsell.

Can you fix a tenancy someone else set up?

Frequently what we're asked to do. A tenancy review covers MFA coverage, legacy authentication, sharing settings, admin accounts and licensing you may be paying for and not using — that last one often pays for the work.

Should we use Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace?

Whichever fits how you already work. Microsoft 365 suits businesses heavy on Office documents, needing Teams calling, or with compliance requirements. Google Workspace suits businesses living in browsers and collaborating in real time. Migrating between them for its own sake is rarely worth the disruption.
